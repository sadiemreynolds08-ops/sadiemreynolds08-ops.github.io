My Story

My junior year of highschool I was practicing volleyball when my coach came up to me and asked "are you eating?" my response was "ya, I just ate a big mac before I got here." Not knowing what he meant at the time I rolled it off my shoulder and kept practicing. Later I went home and explained the strange conversation to my mom. She was also concerned for me and said that she has noticed that I have lost quite a but of weight and that I sleep too much. We decided to go to the doctor and get my bloodwork done. The results came back and I was diagnosed with celiac disease. That rocked my world. I developed an eating disorder and an unhealthy relationship with food because all I knew was gluten filled foods. It took me a couple of years and a lot of research before I developed a healthy relationship with being gluten free. I started finding and making recipes and sharing with my family and friends. I got really good feedback and that is why I decided to start a gluten free bakery. I have created amazing recipes that taste so good that you think you're eating gluten. I hope you enjoy these baked goods as much as I do.
