My Story

In 2015 I was diagnosed with celiac disease and my world was turned upside down. Gluten free food at the time was so unappetizing that I chose stomach pain over a bad meal. After a year of not following a strict gluten free diet, I decided to fully commit to gluten free and it wasn’t nearly as hard as I had feared. What I missed most was baked goods, so I set out to find gluten free recipes that actually tasted good. I shared them with family and friends and got such great feedback. That is how Sans Gluten was born.
